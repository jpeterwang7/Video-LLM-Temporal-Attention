Attention labelling

    How to label
    1. Watch the video, then read the question.
    2. Below, click every time step a person needs to see to answer. Leave out steps that only repeat what you already know.
    3. For each step you picked, drag a box around the object or region that carries the answer. Several boxes are fine; mark the minimum, never background.
    4. If you can't tell what is needed, tick "Unsure" and say why.
    5. Label each video on its own, even when you recognise its reversed twin.

    1. Which time steps are needed? Each step is 2 frames, as the model saw them; the first frame is shown.

    2. Where in each needed step? Drag on the frame to draw a box.
